# Technical Team Lead Role

## Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced **Technical Team Lead / Senior Software Engineer** who can take ownership of the technical direction of our Web3 gaming platform.

This role requires a strong combination of:

- Software architecture expertise
- Web3 and blockchain knowledge
- Engineering leadership
- Product collaboration
- Team development

The Technical Team Lead will work closely with founders, product teams, and engineers to design, build, and scale a next-generation GameFi ecosystem.

---

# Responsibilities

## 🏗️ Technical Leadership

The Technical Team Lead will:

- Define and evolve the overall system architecture
- Establish technical strategy and engineering direction
- Make critical decisions regarding technology choices and infrastructure
- Design scalable, secure, and maintainable systems
- Review technical designs and engineering solutions
- Identify technical risks and develop effective solutions
- Establish engineering standards and best practices


---

## ⛓️ Web3 & Platform Architecture

The role will contribute to:

- Blockchain integration strategy
- Smart contract architecture decisions
- NFT marketplace infrastructure
- Digital asset management systems
- Web3 user experience improvements
- Secure blockchain-based platform development

The Team Lead will help ensure blockchain technology enhances gaming experiences without unnecessary complexity for users.


---

## 👨‍💻 Engineering Management & Team Development

Responsibilities include:

- Guide and mentor software engineers
- Review code quality and technical implementation
- Establish development workflows and engineering processes
- Support hiring and building the engineering team
- Manage technical priorities and development milestones
- Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing
- Improve engineering productivity and quality


---

## 🎮 Product & Business Collaboration

The Technical Team Lead will collaborate with:

- Founders
- Product managers
- Game designers
- Blockchain specialists
- Engineering teams

Responsibilities:

- Translate business goals into technical solutions
- Evaluate product requirements from an engineering perspective
- Provide technical insights during product decisions
- Balance speed, scalability, security, and user experience


---

## 🚀 Platform Development

The Team Lead will help drive:

- Game platform development
- NFT marketplace improvements
- AI-powered feature integration
- Backend and cloud infrastructure scaling
- Performance optimization
- Security improvements
