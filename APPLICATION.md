# How To Apply

We are looking for talented engineers and technical leaders who are passionate about building the future of gaming, Web3, blockchain, and AI-powered digital experiences.

If you are interested in joining our team, please provide the following information:

---

# Candidate Information

## Required Information

Please submit:

1. LinkedIn profile

2. GitHub profile

3. Portfolio or personal website (Optional)

4. Previous projects or technical achievements (Optional)

5. Relevant Web3 / blockchain experience (Optional)

6. Gaming industry experience (Optional)

7. Technical leadership experience (if applicable)

8. Brief introduction about yourself and your interest in this project

---

# Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to understand both technical capability and alignment with our long-term vision.

## Step 1 — Initial Introduction

We will review your background, experience, previous projects, and technical expertise.

Discussion topics:

- Your professional experience
- Previous products you have built
- Web3 / gaming experience
- Your interest in our project

---

## Step 2 — Technical Discussion

A technical conversation with our team to understand:

- Engineering experience
- Architecture thinking
- Problem-solving approach
- Technical decision-making process
- Experience with relevant technologies

---

## Step 3 — Final Interview

Final discussion covering:

- Role expectations
- Collaboration style
- Availability
- Compensation
- Long-term partnership opportunities


---

# What We Value

We value candidates who demonstrate:

- Strong technical ownership
- Product-oriented thinking
- Ability to solve complex problems
- Clear communication
- Engineering leadership
- Passion for gaming and emerging technologies
